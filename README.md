## Systematic Macro Strategies

-- [Danish-mbs-rate-neutral-carry](https://github.com/gabsmm18/Danish-mbs-rate-neutral-carry):
  Rate-neutral relative value on Danish mortgage bonds, 1987–2026. Beta-hedging removes the hidden rate exposure of the yield spread (Sharpe 0.34 to 0.86, 1.21 with value timing) and reveals the negative            convexity of callable mortgages.
- [Predicting the Nationalbank](https://github.com/gabsmm18/Predicting-the-Nationalbank)
  A trading signal for Danish rates: an exchange-market pressure index anticipates the direction of Danmarks Nationalbank's unilateral rate moves (38 of 43 since 2001) and significantly predicts cuts and hikes      (logit t = 4.7 and −3.1)
- [Taylor-rule-duration-strategy](https://github.com/gabsmm18/Taylor-rule-duration-strategy): A US Treasury duration strategy driven by Taylor-rule deviations. On 10-year Treasuries it beats buy-and-hold (Sharpe     0.41 vs 0.28) with a third less drawdown; in 2022 it returned +6.1% while long duration lost 6.1%.
- [FX-carry-crash-insurance](https://github.com/gabsmm18/fx-carry-crash-insurance): A G10 FX carry strategy with a momentum crash filter. The filter cuts carry's crash risk (skewness −0.81 to −0.10) and turns       October 2008 from −11.2% into +3.6%, at the cost of a lower Sharpe ratio.
- [Macro-Regimes-Asset-Allocation](https://github.com/gabsmm18/Macro-Regimes-Asset-Allocation): A regime-switching allocation strategy: classifies the economy into growth and inflation regimes and rotates across    assets, backtested with walk-forward machine learning on US data, 2007–2026.

## Macroeconomic Analysis

- [Taylor-rule-tracker](https://github.com/gabsmm18/Taylor-rule-tracker): Estimates monetary policy reaction functions for the Federal Reserve and the ECB from 1987 to 2026, using data from FRED and the ECB Data    Portal. The project measures how each central bank responds to inflation and economic slack, assesses whether the current policy stance is tight or loose relative to the rule, compares the rule-implied path       with market pricing, and runs forward-looking policy scenarios.
- [Okun-law-analysis](https://github.com/gabsmm18/Okun-law-analysis): Estimates Okun's law, the link between output growth and unemployment, for the US, the euro area, Germany, France, Italy, Spain and Denmark.     The project compares how strongly labour markets respond to the business cycle across countries, tests whether the relationship has remained stable over time, and applies the estimates to diagnose current         labour-market conditions.
- [Yield-Curve-Recession-Model](https://github.com/gabsmm18/Yield-Curve-Recession-Model): Estimates the probability of a US recession twelve months ahead from the slope of the Treasury yield curve. The project      tests whether the signal would have worked in real time, using only information available at each date, and examines every yield-curve inversion in the sample to assess the reliability of one of the most widely   followed recession indicators.
